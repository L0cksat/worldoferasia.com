Welcome to the Official "World of Erasia" Webpage!

Welcome one, and welcome all! This is a special place for us as it is a long standing project that I have had, and I have managed to gather together a group of lovely people to make it a reality. This is a world we are creating together and we would like to share it with you all, so without further ado:

What is the World of Erasia?
The world of Erasia is a homebrewed Dungeons and Dragons 5e/5.5e game world. It is the world where all of the adventures of our players will take place.
What is the objective of the World of Erasia?
The objective is to tell and overarching story, and subsequent personal character stories of each of the player characters at the table, in an effect to create some epic moments that will be etched into Erasian lore, and to be shared to all.
How can I access this lore you speak about?
I'm glad you asked! This is one of the main reasons why I decided to create this website so that I and my players can have a place on the Internet where they can look back fondly on the adventures and stories that were told in Erasia. I will try and post NPCs, lore events, countries and general information about the world on the "World" page. You can also listen to the sessions themselves (I have the first five uploaded) on the "Sessions" page.
