Formed from an alliance of noble houses who wanted to extend their dominion beyond Fortress Atoria. The Concord was created to unite resources, soldiers, and political influence into one banner. They view The Wilds not as mystery, but as land to be _claimed, tamed, and taxed_. Their expeditions resemble military campaigns, complete with banners and heralds.
