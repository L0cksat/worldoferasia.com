The Syndicate began as a splinter sect of a major faith, founded by zealots who believed The Wilds hid ancient corruption that must be purged. Over decades, it has grown into a militant missionary order, recruiting inquisitors, crusaders, and mystics. They see colonization not as profit, but as _divine duty_, a holy cleansing of the frontier.
