The Order began centuries ago as a conclave of druids and rangers sworn to protect the balance of nature. When the corporations began carving into The Wilds, the Order reorganized into a faction of its own, seeking to preserve the untamed lands through careful stewardship. They reluctantly compete in the corporate race to ensure the Wilds are _studied, not plundered_.
