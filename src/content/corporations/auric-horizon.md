Founded only recently by a coalition of disillusioned merchants, explorers, and visionaries, the Auric Horizon Company represents _a break from tradition_. Where the other corporations are bound by greed, nobility, zealotry, or dogma, the Horizon seeks to carve a new destiny in The Wilds — guided by ambition and freedom. Though lacking wealth and influence, they offer something the others cannot: the promise that those who risk everything under their banner will rise as legends in their own right.
