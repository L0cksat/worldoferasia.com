Founded by the Rivenshield family of merchants, the guild began as a caravan company that secured trade across hostile lands. When The Wilds became accessible, Rivenshield pivoted from trade to _resource conquest_, aiming to monopolize rare ores, timber, and relics. They bankroll entire militias and view exploration as just another investment.
